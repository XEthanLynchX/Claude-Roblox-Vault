# Main Objective Brainstorm (2026-10-16)

Playtest finding: spotting anomalies as the main goal wasn't fun. Anomalies become **scares**; players need a real job to do.

## Options considered
1. **Janitor chore list** ✅ *chosen*: mop spills, empty trash, fix lights, lock classrooms. Finishing the list earns the key to the next wing. Fits the fiction (you're the night janitors, the Closer is the "other" janitor).
2. **Restore power:** find fuses around the wing and bring them to the breaker room to power the elevator/stairwell. Strong, but a classic fetch quest (Doors, Pressure, FNAF-style).
3. **Find the missing janitors:** collect belongings of the janitors who "quit" (badge, radio, keys). Great for lore; works better as a side collectible.
4. **Lock-up rounds:** lock every door in the wing while the Closer unlocks them behind you. Fun tension; folded into option 1 as a chore type.
5. **Survive until the bell:** hold out until a timer runs out. Too passive; players hide instead of exploring.

## Why chores win
- Clear, readable goal for new players ("finish the clipboard").
- Splitting chores pushes players **apart**, which is where scares and the Closer hit hardest.
- Chores give the Closer something to **undo** (re-spill, unlock, smash a fixed light), which is dread without a chase.
- Hallucinations can create **fake chores**: a spill only you see that lures you somewhere alone.
- Easy to vary per wing (gym, cafeteria, basement each get their own chore types).
- Option 3 (missing janitors' belongings) is kept as an optional collectible for lore and the achievement journal.
