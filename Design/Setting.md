# Setting: the School

- Players are overnight janitors. Around 2 a.m. the hallway starts looping.
- Mood: fluorescent lights, waxed floors, quiet hum, emergency exit signs.

## Wings (one signature monster each, see [[Design/Monsters]])
1. Main Hall: The Hall Monitor
2. Gym: The Mascot
3. Cafeteria: The Lunch Lady
4. Library: The Overdue
5. Science Wing: The Specimen
6. Music & Auditorium: The Conductor
7. Nurse's Office & Basement: The Nurse
8. Principal's Office (finale): The Principal

## Room themes (randomized per room)
- Classroom (most common): desks (chairs sometimes stacked up for the night), bookshelf, globe, flag, plant, posters
- Science lab: lab benches with sinks, stools, glowing beakers, periodic table
- Art room: big tables, easels, paint splatters, student drawings
- Music room: chairs in arcs, music stands, piano, drums
- Library: rows of tall bookshelves, reading tables, QUIET sign
- Computer lab: rows of computers, some screens glowing ("Are you still there?")
- ~10% of rooms are left completely dark

## Hallway dressing
- Lockers, water fountains, trophy cases, vending machines, benches, student art walls, lost & found
- Posters, bulletin boards, fire extinguisher cabinets, banners overhead
- Floor clutter: papers, backpacks, wet floor signs, knocked-over chairs
